# The carols

The 128 carols of The Snow Globe, in the collection's order (the music box plays them shuffled, every
one before any repeats). Only music free everywhere: its makers (composers and arrangers) died before
1926 and it was published before 1931. The words are not used. Where a harmony's maker is too recent or not named in a modern source, the tune alone plays,
doubled at the octave ("tune" below); otherwise the four parts ("parts").

The music was read from Benjamin Bloomfield's collection of Christmas carols (LilyPond), which its
maker gives to the public domain, and arranged for the music box: the parts an octave up, chords
rolled from the bass up, twelve voices on the MEGA65's four SIDs.
